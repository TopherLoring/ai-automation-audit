# AI Automation Audit & Rescue — draft contract

Not approved for sale or customer data. Both prices are unvalidated. Target: owners of production n8n workflows with recurring failures or rework. Entry $290: one redacted n8n JSON export, maximum 25 nodes; static analysis. Expanded $590: up to three exports, maximum 60 nodes combined, cross-workflow review and more detailed failure analysis. These proposed limits require mock validation. Larger work requires a separate quote.

Deliverables: standalone HTML/PDF and structured JSON with executive summary, scope, findings, severity, confidence, verification, evidence, priorities, confirmation requests and limitations. Human review of high-severity findings. Customer provides version, intended behavior, known symptoms, redacted export and written authorization. Only tested supported node types and versions qualify. No Make/Zapier, production writes, repairs, execution tests, security certification or guaranteed outcomes. External controls and runtime behavior cannot be established from exports alone. Repairs and retests require separate agreement. Acceptance: both artifacts cover agreed scope.

## Proposed terms for owner and customer review
Prepaid invoice using an existing provider; never collect payment credentials. Begin only after payment and written authorization. Target delivery three business days after complete intake, subject to scope confirmation; unmeasured. One factual or clarity correction round requested within five business days of delivery; no new analysis, implementation or retest included. Acceptance on written confirmation or after five business days without a reported material nonconformity, subject to signed agreement. Before work begins, cancellation refund less disclosed nonrefundable processing fees; once work begins, discuss proportional refund for undelivered work. Correct material nonconformities or agree on a refund. These are draft terms requiring owner review and customer agreement; they are not legal advice. Delete data per agreed retention schedule.
